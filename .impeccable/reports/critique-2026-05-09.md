## Anti-Patterns Verdict

### LLM assessment (Assessment A)

**Mostly NOT AI slop, with three significant slips that undercut the system's own brand promise.**

The implementation largely honors DESIGN.md's discipline (Open Sans + weight contrast, navy/blue/off-white restraint, pill CTAs, Radix sheet semantics, reduced-motion handling). The bottom-anchored sheet with up-cast shadow is a distinctive named pattern, the editorial desktop hero (40–56px clamp headline, balance/pretty wrapping, rotated category block, Signal-Blue word-level highlight) is recognizably crafted, and the TikTok-feed metaphor with axis-locked wheel/touch navigation is genuinely different from "AI feature-card grid."

The slips that move it toward AI:

- **Side-stripe pull-quote** at `LaunchpadApp.tsx:1080` (`borderLeft: 3px solid ${BLUE}`). DESIGN.md §6 explicitly bans this; impeccable's absolute-bans list catches the exact pattern. Single most damaging brand-vs-implementation gap.
- **Decorative gradients on photo overlays** at `LaunchpadApp.tsx:1548, 1827, 1942, 2094` (90° dual-vignette on `ImmersiveBackdrop`, bottom-vignette on media cards). The "darkroom gradient on every hero image" pattern. Not in the elevation vocabulary.
- **Glassmorphism beyond the one allowed surface** — `backdrop-filter: blur(...)` on FormatBadge (line 2163), DesktopRailBtn (2290), MobileRailBtn (2331), SearchModal (2532). DESIGN.md sanctions it on exactly one (the learn-more overlay).
- **Hard-coded JS color constants** at `LaunchpadApp.tsx:58–64` duplicate the CSS custom properties. Freezes the home feed against any future theming.
- **Em dashes in user-facing copy** — 7 across the file, most in user-visible strings ("Saved · check back anytime", "Item N / N", search empty state). PRODUCT.md says no em dashes.

**The "would you believe it" test:** If shown the home feed at full size in isolation — no, this is too specific. If shown the side-stripe pull-quote and the dual-vignette media card alone — yes, those scream AI default.

## Overall Impression

Career LaunchPAD has a **strong vocabulary and a leaky implementation**. PRODUCT.md and DESIGN.md set a coherent, distinctive bar — "The Lit Switchboard" with editorial discipline, named rules, anti-references — and the learn-more sheet honors it almost completely. The home feed reaches for the same vocabulary but ships specific spec violations (side-stripe, glassmorphism creep, hard-coded color constants, decorative gradients) that any reader of the spec will catch. The single biggest opportunity is **closing the spec-to-implementation gap on the home feed** — there's no need to redesign anything, just remove the AI defaults that crept in around the edges. Do that and the score moves from 26/40 toward the mid-30s without changing the design language at all.

## What's Working

1. **The learn-more sheet is the system's strongest surface.** `LearnMorePanel.tsx:91–245` + `globals.css:181–555`. Radix Dialog gives `role="dialog"`, `aria-modal`, focus trap, focus return, and Escape-close for free. `Dialog.Title` is wired (line 171), the body scroll-lock saves and restores body position rather than just `overflow:hidden` (preventing scroll jump on close), and the named-section structure (Why it matters / How it connects / Key takeaway / Related) is exactly what PRODUCT.md asks for. This is rare to see implemented this cleanly. **Don't touch it.**

2. **Typographic discipline at the desktop hero.** `LaunchpadApp.tsx:1029–1052`. Clamp-scaled 40–56px headline, balance/pretty wrapping, `HighlightedTitle` (line 2106) doing word-level Signal Blue highlighting on the longest word. The rotated `-2deg` category block (line 1009) adds personality without reaching for a second typeface. This is the Weight-Over-Family Rule executed well.

3. **Reduced-motion respect at the keyframe level.** `globals.css:539–554` plus `LaunchpadApp.tsx:262–272, 1372, 1400–1415`. Sheet animation degrades to 1ms position-stable fade; feed-deck transition collapses; scroll nudge is suppressed. This is the level of motion hygiene PRODUCT.md commits to and most products skip.

## Priority Issues

### [P1] Side-stripe pull-quote violates the system's own ban

- **What:** `LaunchpadApp.tsx:1080` ships `borderLeft: 3px solid ${BLUE}` on the desktop pull-quote.
- **Why it matters:** Directly violates the Tinted-Border Rule in DESIGN.md §2 ("Never a colored side-stripe") and the §6 explicit Don't ("Don't use border-left or border-right greater than 1px as a colored stripe accent"). Both Assessment A and the deterministic detector flagged this independently — strongest convergent signal in the report. Anyone reading the spec will conclude the team isn't enforcing it.
- **Fix:** Replace with one of the in-spec patterns: an uppercase 11px Signal-Blue eyebrow ("Why it matters" style) above the quote on a flat surface; or the existing callout-takeaway tile (Pale Sky `#e6f4ff` fill, 1px Signal Blue at 22% alpha border — DESIGN.md §5).
- **Suggested command:** `$impeccable distill`

### [P1] Glassmorphism leaked beyond the one allowed surface

- **What:** `backdrop-filter: blur(...)` on FormatBadge (`LaunchpadApp.tsx:2163`), DesktopRailBtn (line 2290), MobileRailBtn (line 2331), SearchModal (line 2532). DESIGN.md allows it only on the learn-more overlay.
- **Why it matters:** Glassmorphism on small chrome is the most stereotypically AI-rendering choice in 2024–2026 work. The system claims discipline; the chrome quietly walks it back. SearchModal is at least defensible as a focus-claim surface, but the three rail/chrome uses are decorative.
- **Fix:** Remove `backdrop-filter` from FormatBadge, DesktopRailBtn, MobileRailBtn. Replace with a flat solid surface — navy at 0.62 alpha (already in use as the base background on DesktopRailBtn) without the blur, or the Pure-White pill with 1px Slate-Frame-Mid border per the DESIGN.md icon-button spec. Re-evaluate SearchModal: keep blur or move to flat scrim.
- **Suggested command:** `$impeccable quieter`

### [P1] 9-chip category rail breaks Miller's Law and contradicts "ambient discovery"

- **What:** Desktop and mobile both render For You + 8 category chips inline, always visible. Mobile uses short labels (`categoryShortLabel`, line 242) but still shows all 9.
- **Why it matters:** Above the 4-item working-memory threshold (cognitive load checklist failure on "Minimal choices" and "Progressive disclosure"). Reads as taxonomy chrome on a page that strategically wants to feel uncluttered and editorial. PRODUCT.md says "ambient discovery" — a permanent 9-chip rail is the opposite. Casey (the distracted mobile student in the persona section below) sees 9 chips before the headline.
- **Fix:** Show 4–5 chips inline (For You + the top 3–4 by weight or by user history) and hide the rest behind a "More" / overflow chip with a popover. Alternative: move category filtering into the search experience entirely and let the home stage be content-only above the fold.
- **Suggested command:** `$impeccable distill` (then `$impeccable layout` if the rail relocates)

### [P1] Hard-coded JS color constants duplicate CSS variables

- **What:** `LaunchpadApp.tsx:58–64` defines `STAGE_BG, INK, INK2, BORDER, NAVY, BLUE, SURFACE` as JS constants. Same values exist in `globals.css:8–48` as CSS custom properties.
- **Why it matters:** Token discipline is the load-bearing claim of DESIGN.md. Today, half the system reads from `--navy / --primary-blue / --border-1` (CSS file + LearnMorePanel) and the other half reads from JS constants (home feed). Any future change — dark mode, contrast bump, brand refresh — requires edits in two places, and they will fall out of sync.
- **Fix:** Replace JS constants with `var(--navy)`, `var(--primary-blue)`, etc., applied via inline `style` strings (already valid syntax). Or extract a single token util that reads from `getComputedStyle(document.documentElement)` once. The 20+ inline `'#fff'` / `'#000'` literals in this file should also be reviewed in the same pass — most should resolve to `var(--white)` / `var(--off-white)` or appropriate tokens.
- **Suggested command:** `$impeccable adapt` (pulls inline styles to token references; not a strict semantic fit but the closest available; alternatively `$impeccable harden` if treated as a robustness/portability problem)

### [P2] No image error / loading state for YouTube thumbnails

- **What:** Every `<img>` in `LaunchpadApp.tsx` (FeedMediaDeck immersive backdrop, search results) and in `LearnMorePanel.tsx:163` ships with no `onError`, no `loading="lazy"`, no skeleton, no fallback. The system depends on `img.youtube.com` reachability per `next.config.ts`.
- **Why it matters:** This is a content-first product whose hero on every screen is a video thumbnail. A YouTube CDN hiccup or a removed video displays a black box at desktop full-bleed; `ImmersiveBackdrop` then blurs that black square and gradients overlay it. Students see a malfunction with no recovery affordance. Heuristic 9 (Error Recovery) is the weak heuristic for this exact reason. Riley (stress tester) will find this on day one.
- **Fix:** Add `onError` handler on every `<img>` swapping to a category-tinted SVG fallback (the brand mark scaled up over `CATEGORY_BLOCK_BG[primaryCategory]` would be on-system). Add a 1-frame skeleton via `background: var(--bg-3)` until `onLoad`. Add `loading="lazy" decoding="async"`.
- **Suggested command:** `$impeccable harden` *(deferred per user instruction; flagged for the post-conference batch)*

## Persona Red Flags

Selected per PRODUCT.md's audience (Canadian middle/high school students in ambient myBlueprint moments) plus the standard archetypes most relevant to a content-discovery surface.

### Casey (Distracted Mobile User) — primary persona

Walking the primary action (open Career LaunchPAD on phone in a 5-minute hallway moment, find one thing worth watching, tap into it):

- **9 category chips at the top** before the headline. On a phone in the thumb zone? They're not in the thumb zone — they're at the top, where Casey can't reach with the hand holding the phone. The primary action is mid-screen-and-below, but the taxonomy is at the top.
- **Floating action rail** (Save / Share / Info / Scroll hint) crowds the bottom-right thumb zone alongside the centered Learn More CTA. Three "do something" affordances within a thumb's reach when the strategy commits to "easy to skim, easy to leave."
- **No state persistence verification.** If Casey gets interrupted by a class bell mid-feed, returns later — does scroll position restore? popstate handles deep-linked sheets but not feed scroll depth (worth verifying).
- **No 3G fallback.** YouTube thumbnails on every screen, no skeleton, no progressive loading. School Wi-Fi is famously bad.
- **No labels on the floating rail icons.** Casey is one-handed, distracted, and three-second-attention. Three unlabeled icon buttons in the thumb zone is friction.

### Jordan (Confused First-Timer) — primary persona

Walking the primary action (encounter Career LaunchPAD ambiently inside myBlueprint, figure out what it is, decide whether to engage):

- **Zero contextual help.** No primer, no "what is this," no first-run hint beyond the 420ms scroll-nudge animation. PRODUCT.md says ambient discovery — but Jordan is trying to figure out what they just stumbled into. Currently nothing on screen explains it.
- **"Item N / N" counter** (line 1073–1075) reads as engagement/completion grammar. Jordan parses this as "I am supposed to finish this list." That's the opposite of the strategic intent.
- **"For you" filter is active by default** but isn't visually distinguished as the active filter for someone who doesn't know what "For you" means in this product. Is it personalized? (No, no auth.) Curated? Random? Jordan can't tell.
- **Format chooser button** is unlabeled (icon-only, line 1199). The product separates Video / Article / Playbook as distinct formats but Jordan doesn't see those labels on the chooser, only on chips below.
- **Keyboard shortcuts advertised but undocumented.** The ⌘K hint in the search trigger is good. The j/k/PageDn/Spacebar shortcuts are not visible anywhere — they exist for Alex (power user) but Jordan can't discover them.

### Sam (Accessibility-Dependent User) — required by AODA + WCAG 2.1 AA bar

Walking the primary action with screen reader and keyboard only:

- **Inline-styled buttons** across `LaunchpadApp.tsx` (CategoryChip, FormatChooser, FormatOption, MobileRailBtn, LearnMoreCta, EmptyState button, SearchModal results, search trigger) lack `:focus-visible` rules. Per the audit, only the desktop overlay rail and the learn-more CTA carry one. AODA blocker.
- **Floating action rail icons** rely on `aria-label` only — that's correct for screen readers, but no visible focus indicator means a sighted keyboard user can't see where focus is.
- **Image alt text** on the learn-more sheet poster (`LearnMorePanel.tsx:163`) is `alt=""` — purely decorative, technically allowed since the title is in the dialog header. But the same thumbnail is the only visual cue that this content is *about* the person/career shown. Consider `alt={item.title}` — borderline, worth deciding deliberately.
- **The `learn-more-format` chip** in the sheet header lowercases the source string then CSS-uppercases it (`text-transform: uppercase`). Screen readers may announce the lowercased source. Worth verifying with VoiceOver.
- **Color contrast not visually verified** — the deterministic browser overlay didn't run, so contrast ratios are unverified at runtime. Slate Body (`#65738b`) on Studio Off-White (`#f6f6ff`) computes to ~4.39:1 — borderline against the 4.5:1 body bar in PRODUCT.md. Worth a measured pass.

### Project-specific persona — "Maya, planning-mindset Grade 11 student in a hallway moment"

Derived from PRODUCT.md.

**Profile:** Grade 11, Toronto-area public school, strong English, no prior search intent for career content. Phone in hand between Math and Spare. Has heard of myBlueprint because the school uses it for course planning.

**Behaviors:** Opens myBlueprint to do something else (course selection); encounters Career LaunchPAD ambiently. Will browse for ~3 minutes if curious, ~30 seconds if not. Will return only if first encounter doesn't feel like homework. Does not want to be onboarded, signed up, or tracked.

**Red flags found:**

- **Engagement-software grammar.** The "Item N / N" counter, the floating rail's three affordances, the always-visible 9-chip taxonomy — collectively read as "you are using a product that wants your attention." PRODUCT.md commits to the opposite: "earn attention without demanding it."
- **No "what is this" answer at first encounter.** The product is anonymous-by-design (no auth, no personalization). For Maya, this is a feature — but she still needs a one-sentence answer to "what am I looking at." Currently there's no such surface.
- **The "Saved · check back anytime" toast** suggests there's a saved-list to come back to. There is not (per Assessment A — `savedIds` exists in localStorage but no surface to revisit them). Promising a feature that doesn't exist is the kind of thing that makes Maya not return.
- **Stock-photo Canadiana risk** — not currently violated, but if content seeding leans toward generic photography or broad national imagery, the anti-reference in PRODUCT.md kicks in. Worth a content-side review of the actual data in `src/data/content.ts`.

## Minor Observations

- `EmptyState` (line 2691) uses `borderRadius: 12` on its button. DESIGN.md mandates pill (`9999px`) on all CTAs without exception.
- `Toast` (line 2661) uses `boxShadow: var(--shadow-lg)` — DESIGN.md notes shadow-lg is "reserved for dropdowns, menus, elevated tiles." Toast is none of those. Minor mis-categorization.
- Search modal placeholder ("Search careers, skills, articles…") and feed search trigger ("Search careers, skills…") are inconsistent. Pick one.
- `pullQuoteFor` (line 2741) falls back from `takeaway` to description — when there's no takeaway, the description appears twice on the desktop hero. Worth deduping or hiding the pull-quote in that case.
- Format icon mapping (`LearnMorePanel.tsx:20`) maps `playbook` to `BookOpen` — same icon as `article`. Two formats the spec separates share an icon.
- `DesktopRailBtn` (2249) and `MobileRailBtn` (2300) live as twins. Worth extracting a single `RailButton` with a `variant` prop (maintainability, not design).
- `LaunchpadApp.tsx` is a 2746-line single client component. Bundle size + onboarding-for-contributors concern. Out of scope for design critique but flagged.

## Provocative Questions

1. **What if the category rail disappeared until invoked?** PRODUCT.md says "ambient discovery." A 9-chip taxonomy bar pinned to every screen is destination-software grammar, not ambient grammar. Imagine the desktop with no chips above the fold — just headline, video, Learn More — and a single typographic "All paths" button that opens the rail. Does that read as more confident or less?

2. **Does the home feed need both an immersive backdrop and a media tile?** The desktop currently blurs the thumbnail to 18px with an 0.82-opacity dark gradient under a sharp media tile of the same image. Two layers, same content, doing antagonistic visual work. What does the headline-and-tile-only version look like?

3. **Why is "Item N / N" on screen?** This is engagement-dashboard grammar in a product that's explicitly trying not to be one. The existing `ChevronsDown` "Scroll for more" affordance already communicates "feed continues." What does removing the counter do to the emotional valence?
