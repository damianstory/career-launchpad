# Career LaunchPAD Strategy

## Target problem

Canadian students in course-selection, pathway, or post-secondary planning moments rarely go looking for career education on purpose. The hard part is earning their attention before the question feels urgent, especially when traditional career resources feel stale, fragmented, or like homework.

## Our approach

Career LaunchPAD meets students inside myBlueprint moments with ambient discovery instead of asking them to visit a resource library. We win by turning current, school-sanctioned career content into a polished, low-pressure experience that can lead students from curiosity into deeper articles and playbooks without forcing a planning workflow too early.

## Who it's for

**Primary:** Planning-mindset student - They're hiring Career LaunchPAD to discover modern careers, industries, skills, and next-step ideas without needing to know what to search for.

## Key metrics

- **Entry click-through rate** - Clicks from myBlueprint placements divided by impressions; measured from placement/link analytics.
- **Meaningful session rate** - Sessions with at least 2 minutes of activity or multiple content interactions; measured in product analytics.
- **Content completion rate** - Video completions at 80% watched plus article/playbook depth signals; measured through custom engagement events.
- **Save/share rate** - Lightweight content saves or shares per session and per content item; measured in product analytics.
- **Return visit rate** - Returning browser sessions within 30 days where measurable without authentication.

## Tracks

### Ambient discovery

Make entry points and the first screen compelling enough that students engage in moments where they are already using myBlueprint.

_Why it serves the approach:_ This is the core bet: students should not have to decide to "go do career exploration" before the product can help.

### Content depth and trust

Shape videos, articles, and playbooks into current, authentic, Canadian-relevant career content that feels useful without becoming an assignment.

_Why it serves the approach:_ Discovery only works if the content earns attention quickly and gives students a credible next layer when they want more.

### Engagement signals

Instrument the experience so the team can tell which entry points, formats, topics, and depth patterns actually hold student attention.

_Why it serves the approach:_ The first version needs proof of student pull before investing in heavier planning, reporting, or personalization systems.

## Milestones

- **2026-05-28 to 2026-05-29** - Skills Canada Conference: have a conference-ready v1 that demonstrates the new student-facing discovery experience and the engagement signals it captures.

## Not working on

- Authentication, account-based personalization, admin CMS, or an algorithmic recommendation engine.
- Educator, school, or board reporting dashboards.
- Interactive planning workflows such as saved plans, checklists, worksheets, or pathway builders.

## Marketing

**One-liner:** Career LaunchPAD is a student-first career discovery layer for the myBlueprint moments where future decisions already start.
